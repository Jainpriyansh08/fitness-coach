## 1. Reading the intake

Read every answer, including free text. Extract into these buckets and label each item
**[Client-stated] / [Confirmed in consult] / [Working hypothesis] / [Needs clinical confirmation] / [Missing]**:

| Bucket         | Look for                                                                                                       | Watch out for                                                                                                          |
| -------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Profile        | Age, sex, height, weight, waist, occupation, daily activity, city/climate                                      | Missing height/weight (fine — don't guess), sedentary desk job + "very active" self-rating                             |
| Goals          | Primary/secondary goals, the _why_, timeline/event, what success feels like                                    | Goal–timeline mismatch (e.g. 15 kg in 8 weeks), goal stated only as a scale number                                     |
| Health         | Conditions, medicines, injuries, surgeries, pain, symptoms, doctor advice                                      | "Prefer to discuss" (= must ask privately), ticked symptom boxes, medicines without a listed condition (or vice versa) |
| Women's health | Pregnancy, postpartum (time since delivery, type), breastfeeding, cycle, peri/menopause, pelvic-floor symptoms | Postpartum appearance goals that may involve more than fat (see §5B)                                                   |
| Training       | Experience, current activity, enjoyment, dislikes, injuries from training, equipment, setting                  | "Training regularly" but 0 days/week now; equipment listed vs. what they'll actually use                               |
| Practicalities | Days/week, minutes/session, best times, travel/shift work, confidence in schedule                              | Confidence ≤ 6/10 → plan for the busy week, not the ideal one                                                          |
| Food           | Pattern (veg/egg/non-veg/vegan/Jain), allergies/intolerances, who cooks, eating out, meals/day, difficulties   | Family cooks (→ plate/portion strategy, not separate meals), "skip breakfast", late dinners, large portions, cravings  |
| Typical day    | Actual meals, drinks, snacks, sugar in tea, oil, water, alcohol, supplements                                   | Under-reporting (oil, tea sugar, snacks), very low protein (common), self-started supplements/remedies                 |
| Recovery       | Sleep hours/quality, stress, tracking comfort, plan style preference                                           | < 6 h sleep, high stress, "prefer simple habits" (→ flexible nutrition)                                                |
| Adherence      | What made consistency hard before, past attempts                                                               | Same barrier listed again for this plan's schedule                                                                     |

Then check **inconsistencies** (e.g. "no health conditions" but takes metformin; "vegetarian" but
eats eggs; 5 days/week planned with 30% confidence) — list them as things to clarify kindly.

## 2. The five-section response

Keep it scannable — the coach may read it minutes before the call.

### 1. Coach's client briefing

```
**Snapshot:** <age> · <sex> · <occupation/activity> · <training status> · <setting & equipment> · <time available>
**Goals:** Primary — … · Secondary — … · Why — … · Timeline — … → Realistic? <yes/partly/no + numbers>
**Strengths:** 3–5 bullets (habits, resources, motivation, support)
**Challenges:** training · nutrition · lifestyle · adherence · inconsistencies to clarify
**Health considerations:** each with label [Client-stated]/[Needs clinical confirmation]/…
**Bottom line:** one or two sentences: who this person is and what will make or break the plan
```

Realism check examples: fat loss ~0.5–1% of body weight per week is a sustainable range for most
people with excess weight; beginners can gain strength fast in the first 8–12 weeks; muscle gain is
slow (expect visible change over months). Postpartum, thyroid, PCOS and medications can change the
pace — say so without blaming the condition for everything.

### 2. Medical & safety research

```
**Triage:** <A/B/C/D> — <one-line reason>. Goes ahead: … · Deferred until cleared: … (or "nothing")
**Ask on the call:** the safety questions that decide the triage
Then for each relevant condition (template in research-protocol.md §5), with citations.
```

### 3. First-meeting consultation script

Timed flow (section 3 below) + 8–12 prioritised question cards:

```
**Q<n>. "<exact conversational wording>"**
Why it matters: … · Listen for: … · Optional follow-up: "…" (only if …) · Affects: training / nutrition / lifestyle / safety / adherence
```

Order questions so the conversation flows: goals → health → training → nutrition → expectations.
Mark the 3–4 must-ask questions with ★. Never re-ask what the intake answers unless it needs
clarification — instead _reflect it back_ ("You mentioned evenings are best — is that still true on
weeks when …?").

### 4. What I must establish before programming

```
| Must know | Ask on call | Needs clinician after | Why it matters |
```

Plus a 6–10 item tick-box **meeting checklist** the coach can glance at during the call.

### 5. My recommended coaching direction (PROVISIONAL)

Training direction (frequency, split, emphasis, what to avoid for now) · Nutrition direction
(style, protein anchor, deficit or not) · Lifestyle priorities (2–3) · First 2-week focus ·
What would change this direction. Mark clearly: _provisional — not a prescription._

## 3. Consultation flow (40–50 minutes)

| Time  | Segment                   | Aim                                                                 | Coach tips                                                                    |
| ----- | ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 0–5   | Opening & rapport         | Comfort, how coaching works                                         | 2–3 natural opening lines (below); explain "plan → weekly check-ins → adjust" |
| 5–13  | Goals & motivation        | The real goal and why now; success beyond the scale                 | Ask for a picture of a good day 3 months from now                             |
| 13–23 | Health & readiness        | Safety, conditions, meds, pain, restrictions                        | Only the sensitive questions genuinely needed; offer to skip or discuss later |
| 23–33 | Training & lifestyle      | Experience, preferences, time, equipment, barriers                  | Design for the busiest realistic week                                         |
| 33–43 | Nutrition & routine       | What and how they eat, who cooks, eating out, willingness to change | Ask what they _don't_ want to change                                          |
| 43–50 | Expectations & next steps | First steps, check-ins, communication                               | Agree the first 2 weeks and the check-in day                                  |

**Opening line examples** (adapt to the client):

- "Thanks for filling in the form so carefully — I've read all of it, so today isn't about repeating it. I'd like to understand what matters most to you and what your weeks really look like."
- "The way I coach: we start with a plan that fits your actual life, check in every week, and adjust based on what really happens — not on a perfect version of you."
- "Some questions are about health. Answer only what you're comfortable with; anything sensitive we can talk about separately."

## 4. Question bank

Adapt wording to the client. Pick the ones the intake leaves open.

**Goals & motivation**

- "What would make you feel these next three months were genuinely successful?" (listen for non-scale outcomes: energy, clothes, strength, confidence, health markers)
- "Why now — what made you reach out this month?" (event, health scare, life change)
- "If the scale didn't move but you felt stronger and more energetic, how would you feel?" (reveals scale-dependence; ED-risk signals)
- "What have you tried before? What worked, even for a while — and what made it stop?"

**Health & readiness** (only what's needed)

- "Is there anything about your health or recovery that makes certain exercises uncomfortable?"
- "Has any doctor ever told you to avoid or limit any kind of activity?"
- "When you're active, have you ever had chest discomfort, felt faint, very breathless, or your heart racing in a way that worried you?" (screening — any yes → see safety-screening.md)
- "You mentioned <condition/medicine>. Who looks after that, and when was it last reviewed?"
- "Are you on any medicines or supplements I should know about, including ones you buy yourself?" (interactions with remedies)
- Women, if relevant and comfortable: cycle regularity, pregnancy plans, postpartum details, "any leaking, heaviness or pain with coughing, jumping or lifting?"

**Training & lifestyle**

- "On your busiest week, what would training realistically look like?"
- "What's usually the first thing that makes it difficult for you to stay consistent?"
- "What kind of exercise have you actually enjoyed — even as a kid?"
- "Where will you train, at what time, and what's there?" (home space, equipment, gym hours/crowding)
- "How do you feel about tracking — steps, food, weight — energising or stressful?"

**Nutrition & routine**

- "Walk me through yesterday from waking up — what did you eat and drink, roughly how much?"
- "Who cooks at home, and how open is the family to small changes?"
- "What parts of your current eating routine would you prefer not to change?"
- "When do cravings or overeating usually happen?" (time, emotion, situation)
- "How many teas/coffees a day, with sugar? Any alcohol? Any fasting days?"

**Expectations & next steps**

- "How would you like to check in — and what's the best day?"
- "If you miss a few days, what would you want me to do — nudge you or give you space?"
- "Out of 10, how confident are you about the first two weeks we just agreed?" (< 7 → shrink the plan)

## 5. Special cases (use only when relevant)

### A. Thyroid disease

Don't treat "thyroid" as one condition. Find out: hypothyroidism, Hashimoto's, hyperthyroidism/
Graves', postpartum thyroiditis, other, or unconfirmed. Ask respectfully: who diagnosed it and
when; is it treated, with what; when was it last reviewed (don't interpret the test); any ongoing
fatigue, dizziness, palpitations, heat/cold intolerance or exercise-tolerance problems; any doctor
restrictions; recent weight change. Don't attribute every weight change to the thyroid; don't
claim any exercise, diet, detox or supplement "fixes" the thyroid. Uncontrolled hyperthyroid
symptoms (racing heart, tremor, heat intolerance, weight loss) → defer vigorous training until
the doctor confirms it is controlled. Details: conditions-primer.md.

### B. Post-pregnancy abdominal concerns

Don't assume it's only fat. Ask: time since delivery; vaginal or caesarean; complications or
persistent pain; whether the doctor has cleared/limited activity; breastfeeding; sleep and
recovery; abdominal discomfort, doming/bulging or concern about separation; pelvic-floor
symptoms (leaking, heaviness, pain); activity already resumed; what they personally want to
improve (comfort, strength, appearance, function, energy). Explain that postpartum abdominal
appearance has several causes (abdominal wall, skin, posture, fat). No promises of targeted
belly-fat loss; no automatic crunches, planks, intense HIIT or aggressive deficits; consider
breastfeeding energy needs, hydration, sleep and mood. Pelvic-floor symptoms, doming that doesn't
improve, pain, or C-section wound concerns → pelvic-health physiotherapist / obstetric follow-up.
Build the return-to-training gradually and only when enough is known.

### C. Injuries or persistent pain

Find: location, how long, what triggers/eases it, any diagnosis, treatment, current restrictions,
what daily activities are limited, what is currently tolerated. Don't diagnose the cause; don't
prescribe "corrective exercise" as treatment. Plan around confirmed restrictions using tolerated
movements and gradual progression; refer to a physiotherapist/doctor for anything unclear,
worsening, or with red flags (safety-screening.md).

### D. Adherence struggles

Explore behaviour, environment and psychology — never assume lack of discipline. Cover work
schedule, family responsibilities, money, food availability, sleep, motivation, past attempts,
confidence, and how they recover from missed days. Build a minimum-effective routine (e.g. 2 ×
20-minute sessions + daily walks) that survives the worst realistic week, then add.

### E. Others that change the consultation

- **Pregnancy (current):** the obstetric care provider leads; coaching supports only within their
  guidance. Ask about any provider restrictions and warning signs.
- **Possible disordered eating** (rigid rules, bingeing, compensating, intense fear of weight gain,
  very low intake): don't push tracking, weighing or deficits; gently suggest professional support.
- **Medication for weight/diabetes (e.g. semaglutide, tirzepatide, insulin, sulfonylureas):** ask
  who manages it, appetite and side-effects, hypoglycaemia history.
- **Older adults:** falls, balance, bone health, joint replacements, medications.
- **Neurodivergence (e.g. ADHD):** simple structure, reminders, novelty, short sessions.

## 6. LIVE COACH MODE and post-consult updates

**LIVE COACH MODE** reply format (≤ 4 lines):

```
Insight: <the most important thing in that answer>
Ask next: "<exact question>"
Safety: <immediate concern, or "none">
Why: <one short reason>
```

**After the consultation** (coach shares notes): update, don't restart.

```
**New / confirmed:** …
**Conflicts with intake:** … (and which to trust)
**Still uncertain:** … (high-priority follow-ups only — max 3)
**Safety update:** triage <unchanged/changed to X> because …
**Ready to plan?** Yes / Yes with PENDING items (…) / Not yet — need …
**Initial strategy:** 3–5 bullets
```

When the coach says **"CONSULTATION COMPLETE"** → go to Stage 3 (Decision Brief, then build).
